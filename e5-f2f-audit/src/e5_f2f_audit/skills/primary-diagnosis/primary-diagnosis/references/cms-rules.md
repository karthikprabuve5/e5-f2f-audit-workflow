# Primary Diagnosis — CMS Audit Rules
# MBPM Pub. 100-02, Chapter 7 | §30.5.1.2, §30.2, §40.2
# ICD-10-CM Official Guidelines FY2026 | 42 CFR §409.42(b)

---

## F2F Documentation Requirement
<!-- cms_section_id: PD_F2F_DOCUMENTATION -->
<!-- element_type: REQUIREMENT -->

Per §30.5.1.2, the F2F encounter documentation must:
- Support that the encounter related to the **primary reason** the patient
  requires home health services
- Contain clinical findings including diagnoses, medical history, and medications
- Be clinically grounded — conclusory statements without clinical findings fail

---

## Primary Diagnosis Specificity Standard
<!-- cms_section_id: PD_SPECIFICITY -->
<!-- element_type: REQUIREMENT -->

The F2F note must name a specific medical condition driving the home health need.

**Acceptable:** Named condition with type, acuity, or anatomic site;
ICD-10 code with description; condition with clear HH nexus.
ICD-10 codes must be to the highest specificity level supported by documentation.

**Not Acceptable:** Conclusory ("patient requires home health"); vague ("heart
disease", "pain", "weakness"); symptom-only when diagnosis is known;
single abbreviation without context ("DM", "HTN", "CVA").

---

## Clinical Relevance — Nexus to Home Health Need
<!-- cms_section_id: PD_CLINICAL_RELEVANCE -->
<!-- element_type: REQUIREMENT -->

Per §30.5.1.2, the F2F diagnosis must be the condition **driving** the
home health episode — not a coincidental or routine condition.

**Nexus established:** F2F documents the same condition for which skilled
services are ordered (e.g., CHF exacerbation → IV diuresis nursing).

**Nexus NOT established:** F2F addresses only a routine stable condition
while home health is ordered for a separate acute condition (e.g., annual
diabetes check when HH is ordered for post-surgical wound care).

**Documentation content is the test — not appointment intent:** If a condition
is discovered and documented during the encounter, the nexus IS met even if
the visit was originally scheduled for a different reason. Evaluate what is
written in the note, not why the patient booked the appointment.

## 485 Alignment
<!-- cms_section_id: PD_POC_ALIGNMENT -->
<!-- element_type: REQUIREMENT -->

Per §40.2, the F2F primary diagnosis must be consistent with the primary
diagnosis on the Plan of Care (485), provided as the system prompt anchor.

**ALIGNED** — same condition at equivalent or higher specificity:
- Exact ICD-10 code match, OR
- Same condition; different documentation style (narrative vs coded), OR
- F2F narrative maps unambiguously to the 485 code

**PARTIALLY_ALIGNED** — clinically related but lower specificity:
- F2F documents "CHF"; 485 codes "I50.31 acute on chronic systolic CHF"

**MISALIGNED** — clinically distinct, no causal relationship:
- F2F: routine hypertension check; 485: wound care for surgical dehiscence

---

## Non-Qualifying Situations
<!-- cms_section_id: PD_NON_QUALIFYING -->
<!-- element_type: ILLUSTRATION -->

1. No diagnosis stated in F2F note
2. Symptom-only when underlying diagnosis is known or documented nearby
3. Coincidental F2F — encounter addresses different condition than HH driver
4. Conclusory language without a named clinical condition
5. Diagnosis misaligned with 485 primary without clinical explanation
