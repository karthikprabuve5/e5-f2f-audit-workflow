## Two-Prong Homebound Test
<!-- cms_section_id: HB_TWO_PRONG -->

### Prong 1 — Functional or Medical Basis — OR Logic
<!-- cms_section_id: HB_CRITERIA_ONE -->
<!-- logic: OR — at least ONE of the following must be present -->
- `device_needed` — Patient needs supportive device: crutches, cane, walker, wheelchair
- `special_transport` — Patient needs special transportation to leave home
- `assistance_of_person` — Patient needs assistance of another person to leave home
- `medically_contraindicated` — Leaving home is medically contraindicated due to condition

### Prong 2 — Effort Standard — AND Logic
<!-- cms_section_id: HB_CRITERIA_TWO -->
<!-- logic: AND — BOTH of the following must be present simultaneously -->
- `normal_inability` — Normal inability to leave home exists
- `considerable_effort` — Leaving home requires considerable and taxing effort

Both prongs must be satisfied for homebound status to be MET.

**Phrase-alone rule (§30.1.1):** Standardized phrases ("taxing effort to leave home")
are neither required nor sufficient on their own. Criterion Two requires clinical
context — diagnosis, duration, functional limitations, clinical course, prognosis,
or therapeutic intervention results — that together establish both sub-criteria.
Phrase + clinical context = STRONG. Phrase alone = WEAK.

## Language Thresholds
<!-- cms_section_id: HB_LANGUAGE_STANDARDS -->
<!-- element_type: EXAMPLE -->

### Strong Language — Passes CMS Standard
- "considerable and taxing effort to leave home due to [condition]"
- "unable to leave without assistance of another person"
- "medically contraindicated to leave home"
- "confined to wheelchair, cannot leave without special transportation"
- "physician restricted activity to [specific limitation]"

### Weak Language — Fails CMS Standard
- "difficulty leaving home" — does not meet effort threshold
- "patient is homebound" — conclusory, no clinical basis
- "does not go out much" — no functional basis
- "elderly and frail" — advanced age alone is non-qualifying
- "patient is weak" — no link to inability to leave home

## Allowable Absences
<!-- cms_section_id: HB_ALLOWABLE_ABSENCES -->

Medical absences — always allowable, never disqualify homebound:
- Outpatient dialysis
- Chemotherapy or radiation therapy
- Adult day care (licensed/certified by State)
- Any health care treatment appointment

Non-medical absences — allowable only if infrequent or short duration:
- Religious services (always deemed infrequent)
- Occasional barber, walk around block, family events
- NOT allowable if frequent or lengthy

## Place of Residence
<!-- cms_section_id: HB_PLACE_OF_RESIDENCE -->

Patient's residence is wherever they make their home:
- Own dwelling, apartment, relative's home, assisted living facility
- NOT a hospital, SNF, or nursing facility under Medicaid

Assisted living facility qualifies as residence if not primarily
providing skilled nursing or therapeutic services to inpatients.
Coverage denied if HHA services duplicate what facility must provide
under State licensure. Multiple residences permitted — homebound
criteria must be met at each location.

---

## Clinical Documentation Standards
<!-- cms_section_id: HB_SUPPORTING_DOCS -->

Medical record must contain documentation that substantiates:
- Patient's homebound status
- Need for skilled services

Homebound documentation must be clinically grounded — not conclusory.
HHA assessment information alone is insufficient — must be corroborated
by physician or acute/post-acute care facility medical record entries.

F2F encounter note must demonstrate:
- Encounter occurred within required timeframe
- Encounter related to primary reason patient requires home health

---

## Non-Qualifying Condition
<!-- cms_section_id: HB_NON_QUALIFYING -->
<!-- element_type: ILLUSTRATION -->

Advanced age alone — feebleness or insecurity from aging without
a documented functional or medical basis — does not qualify.
Patient must still meet at least one Prong 1 criterion.
